# Digital Marketing Performance Analysis

## Overview

A Digital Marketing Performance Analysis project built using Python, Excel, and Power BI to evaluate campaign ROI, channel acquisition efficiency, product profitability, and regional sales distribution.

## Tools & Technologies

- Python (Pandas, NumPy)
- Power BI
- Excel
- Data Cleaning & Preprocessing
- Business Intelligence & Data Visualization

## Key KPIs

- Total Revenue: 310.75M
- Total Profit: 116.89M
- Total Ad Spend: 430.25K
- Total Orders: 35K
- Profit Margin: 37.62%

## Business Insights

- Facebook Ads generated the highest revenue at approximately 96.67M, followed by Google Ads at 72.27M.
- Top-performing campaigns were Fb_Retargeting (36M), Fb_Newcustomers (33M), and Fb_Summer (28M).
- Laptops generated the highest product revenue at approximately 142M and 52M profit, followed by Smartphones at 87M.
- Cairo recorded the highest regional revenue at approximately 95M, followed by Giza and Alexandria at 54M each.
- January recorded the highest advertising spend at approximately 84K.
- Returning customers drove 60.09% of total revenue, while new customers accounted for 39.91%.
